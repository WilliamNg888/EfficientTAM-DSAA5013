## DSAA 5013
Our contributions are in the Files "experiments" and "outputs".

In "experiments", "memory_tailrisk" runs the inference experiment on the dataset DAVIS2017;
"segtrack_v2" preprocesses the new dataset Segtravk_v2, such that we can test EfficientTAM on the new dataset;
"ablation_generalization" reproduces the EfficientTAM paper ablations across six checkpoints and extends the tail-risk / fallback story to the cross-attention, backbone, and resolution axes.
